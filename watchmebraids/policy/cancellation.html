Cancellation Policy

How your subscription works

You are charged $39.99 today, $39.99 again on day 7, and then $39.99 every 30 days automatically, to the same payment method, until you cancel. This is a recurring subscription, not a one-time purchase. The amount never changes on its own.

Charges appear on your statement as WATCHMEBRAID.

We email you before every charge, showing the amount and the billing date. This includes the charge taken on day 7.

How to cancel

You can cancel at any time, at no cost, with no minimum number of payments. Email support@watchmebraids.com with the email address used at checkout and the word “cancel”, at least 24 hours before your next charge. You may also call +1 (888) 353-6118. We confirm every cancellation by email — keep that confirmation as your proof.

Cancelling stops all future charges. Your access continues until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Cancelling after a charge

If a charge has already been taken when you contact us, the cancellation applies to all future charges. To request a refund on the charge already taken, see our Refund Policy.

Pausing instead of cancelling

If you would rather pause than cancel, write to us and we will hold your subscription instead of closing it.

What happens to your access

After cancellation your access remains active until the end of the period you already paid for, then closes. Nothing is charged again.
