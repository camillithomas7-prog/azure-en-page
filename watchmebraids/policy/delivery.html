Delivery and Access

How you receive the program

Watch Me Braid is entirely digital. Nothing is shipped.

Instant access

As soon as your payment is confirmed you receive an email with your access link. In most cases it arrives within a few minutes.

What is included

  • The Watch-Me Video Method — 13 videos, approximately 2 hours 41 minutes
  • Hair Braiding Training Manual — 47-page PDF, instant download
  • Styles Library, Edge Protection and Maintenance guides
  • Unlimited pause, rewind and rewatch while your subscription is active

Devices

The program works on any device with a browser: phone, tablet, laptop or smart TV. No app to install.

If you did not receive your access email

Check your spam and promotions folders first, then email support@watchmebraids.com from the address you used at checkout. We resend access manually within one business day.

Availability

We aim to keep the program available at all times. Brief interruptions for maintenance may occur and do not entitle you to a refund unless access is unavailable for more than seven consecutive days.
