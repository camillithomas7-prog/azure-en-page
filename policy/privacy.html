What we collect

When you place an order we collect your name, email address, shipping and billing address, phone number if you provide it, and the details needed to process your payment. We also collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit on our site.

We never see or store your full card number. Payment data is transmitted directly to our payment processor over an encrypted connection.

Why we collect it, and on what legal basis

To perform our contract with you: processing and shipping your order, charging your subscription renewals, sending order confirmations and renewal reminders, and handling returns. For our legitimate interests: preventing fraud and improving our site. To comply with legal obligations: keeping tax and accounting records. With your consent: marketing emails, where you asked for them.

Marketing emails

If you ticked the box to receive news and offers, we use your email address for that purpose. Every marketing email contains an unsubscribe link. You can withdraw your consent at any time, and doing so does not affect the transactional emails about your orders and renewals.

Who we share it with

We share only what is necessary, and only with the parties that help us run the business: our payment processor, our shipping carriers, our order management platform, and our email provider. Each acts under a written agreement and may use your data only for the service it provides to us. We do not sell your personal information.

International transfers

Because we ship to the United States, some of our providers are located outside the United Kingdom. Where that happens, transfers are covered by the safeguards required under United Kingdom data protection law, such as standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as required by tax and accounting law. Anything not covered by that obligation is deleted on request.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, ask us to restrict how we use it, object to our use of it, or ask us to transfer it to another provider. Write to averaofficial@hotmail.com and we reply within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our reply you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the United Kingdom supervisory authority, at ico.org.uk. Customers in the United States may also have rights under their own state law, and we honour those requests through the same email address.

Cookies

Our site uses cookies to keep your cart, remember your preferences, and measure how the site is used. You can block cookies in your browser settings, but parts of the checkout may stop working.

Children

Our products are not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Changes

If we change this policy we update this page. Material changes are announced by email to customers with an active subscription.
