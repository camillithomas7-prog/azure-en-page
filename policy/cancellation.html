Cancellation Policy

How subscriptions work

Your Avera Club membership is charged today for the amount of the offer you selected, the same amount again seven days later, and the same amount every thirty days thereafter, automatically, to the same payment method, until you cancel. The amount never changes on its own. Charges appear on your payment statement as AVERA.

Reminder before every renewal

We email you before each charge, showing the amount and the billing date, so you always know what is coming. This includes the charge taken seven days after your first order.

How to cancel

You can cancel at any time, at no cost, with no minimum number of orders. Email averaofficial@hotmail.com with your order number and the words "cancel subscription", or call +39 333 778 8734, at least 24 hours before your next renewal date. We confirm every cancellation by email. Keep that confirmation as your proof.

Cancelling after a renewal has been charged

If a renewal has already been charged when you contact us, the cancellation applies to all future charges. To request a refund on the charge already taken, see our Return & Refund Policy.

Pausing instead of cancelling

If you would rather delay your next delivery than cancel, write to us and we will move your renewal date.
