FAQ

What makes Batana oil different from other oils?
It’s a single, pure ingredient — authentic Batana hand-harvested in Honduras — rich in the natural vitamin E, omega 3-6-9, and vitamin A that cheap blends simply don’t have.
Does it really help with thinning edges?
Yes. Batana oil has been used for centuries to restore edges and even areas of alopecia. It stimulates follicles, improves circulation, and helps fill in thinning spots with regular use.
How do I use it?
Massage a small amount into your scalp and edges 3 to 4 times a week. Leave it on overnight, or at least 20 minutes before rinsing. It also works as a daily styling oil.
Is it right for every hair type?
Absolutely. It works on natural, relaxed, and color-treated hair, and on protective styles. It’s gentle, nutrient-rich, and free of parabens, silicones, and sulfates.
Will it make my hair greasy?
No — our oil absorbs quickly and leaves no heavy residue. It nourishes deeply while keeping hair soft.
How soon will I see results?
Most women notice softer, shinier hair within 1 to 2 weeks. Edges and thinning areas often show visible regrowth between 3 and 8 weeks.
Can I use it on my skin too?
Yes! Batana oil is safe for skin: it hydrates dry areas, improves elasticity, and protects against oxidative stress.
