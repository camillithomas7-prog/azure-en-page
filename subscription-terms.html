Subscription Terms

These terms explain exactly how the AVERA auto-refill subscription is billed, how it is delivered, and how you cancel it. By checking “Subscribe & save — automatic refill every 30 days” at checkout, you agree to the terms below.

1. What you are signing up for

  • An auto-refill subscription for Natural Batana Hair Oil, shipped and billed every 30 days.
  • You save 10% off the one-time price on every refill, for as long as the subscription stays active.
  • The recurring amount for the standard pack is $39.99 every 30 days. The exact amount for the pack you select is always shown on the product page, in the cart, at checkout, and in your confirmation email before you pay.

2. Billing

  • Your first charge is taken today, when you place the order.
  • After that, the same payment method is charged automatically every 30 days for the same amount, until you cancel.
  • Charges appear on your bank or card statement as AVERA.
  • We send you an email reminder before every renewal, showing the amount and the billing date, with a one-click link to cancel or skip.
  • The subscription price is locked in. We will never change it without at least 30 days’ written notice by email, and you can cancel before the change takes effect.
  • If a payment fails, we retry it and email you. No product ships until a payment is successfully collected.

3. Shipping

  • Free shipping on every refill. Orders ship within 1 business day of the charge.
  • You can change your delivery address, skip a refill, or reschedule the next delivery at any time from Manage Subscription.

4. Cancellation — anytime, no fees

  • There is no minimum number of orders, no contract, and no cancellation fee.
  • Cancel yourself in seconds from Manage Subscription, or from the link in any of our emails.
  • Or email averaofficial@hotmail.com — we confirm every cancellation by email within 24 hours.
  • Cancel at least 24 hours before the next billing date to avoid that refill being charged.

5. Refunds and guarantee

  • 30-day money-back guarantee on your first order: if it is not for you, email us within 30 days for a full refund.
  • If a refill is charged after you asked to cancel, we refund it in full — no questions asked.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

6. Buying without a subscription

The subscription is optional. Leave the box unchecked to make a single purchase: you are charged once, there is no recurring billing, and nothing is stored for future charges beyond what your payment provider requires for the order.

7. Contact

AVERA — customer care: averaofficial@hotmail.com. We answer within 24 hours, Monday to Friday.
